## Prioritized consolidated findings

1. Planning blocker, raised by design: content scope has no production ceiling. Resolve with explicit first-expedition map and asset caps. This is not a claim that the GBA architecture is blocked.
2. Major, design/zoom-out/comprehension: five stones can become five repeated errands; protagonist motivation and final payoff are undefined. Set story purpose and distinguish expeditions through route and objective design.
3. Major, all relevant lenses: visual identity requires coherent terrain, vegetation, characters, interiors, creatures and battle imagery. Separate the milestone's replacement boundary from the full campaign ambition.
4. Major, code/readiness/structural: inherited story scripts and saved flags require an audit. Use an authoritative acquisition state and test duplicate rewards, failed grants and safe return.
5. Major, code/readiness/design: the runner's emulator states do not validate normal saving. Add an in-game save, process shutdown and resume acceptance check; define compatibility policy.
6. Major, code/design/structural: art scripts are order-sensitive prototypes. Establish clean inputs, repeatable conversion, tool/source versions and per-tileset allocation reports.
7. Major, code/readiness/zoom-out: verify a distributed patch through an independent implementation. Existing IPS size limits need reconsideration if the ROM grows.
8. Minor clarity issue, comprehension/structural: historical updates mix proposals with confirmed goals. The consolidated concepts_brief.md now distinguishes them.

## Synthesis choices

The reviewers broadly agree on completing one small Amazon expedition before expanding. Roster suggestions differed: one reviewer suggested a partner plus two catchable species; another suggested a partner plus one catchable species. The brief recommends the smaller two-species total as a provisional production cap. No user-approved roster was inferred from either recommendation.

The brief proposes three outdoor maps and one interior to turn the shared small-first advice into a reviewable boundary. These counts were not user-confirmed. Exact animal choices, guardians, evolution chains, region order and story payoff remain open.
